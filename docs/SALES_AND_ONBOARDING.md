# Sales demonstration

## Two-minute script

“ClinicFlow aapke business ki website par reception ka initial kaam handle karta hai. Customer services aur timings pooch sakta hai aur appointment request bhej sakta hai.”

1. Show the fictional Pearl Dental Care homepage. Explain that the owner can change branding, services, hours, FAQs and greeting from the dashboard.
2. Ask reception “Clinic kab khulta hai?” Show the configured hours. Ask for a service price that was supplied by the owner. For a missing price, show the unavailable-information response.
3. Submit a request using a fictional name and phone. Say “Ye appointment request hai. Reception isko review karke confirm karega.”
4. Sign in to the owner dashboard, find the request and change it to Contacted. Show that counts come from stored records.
5. Change one introduction sentence or FAQ in Business settings, save and refresh the public website.
6. Open the separate embed test page and show the same reception there.

Close with “Aapke business ki approved information se ek trial demo configure kar sakte hain. Pehle aapke use cases aur reception workflow test karenge, phir live usage ka scope aur charges agree karenge.”

Do not promise a percentage increase in bookings, guaranteed income, fully automated scheduling, medical accuracy, compliance certification or 24/7 availability on free hosting.

## First customer onboarding

1. Choose one appointment-based business whose owner agrees to review a demonstration.
2. Collect approved business name, public contact details, timezone, weekly hours, services, exact supplied prices and FAQs. Confirm which prices must remain unavailable.
3. Ask who will review requests, what counts as a confirmed visit, and which handoff contact may be shown publicly.
4. Create a separate business deployment for the first pilot. Create its owner account and explicit membership. Keep credentials private.
5. Configure all information through the editor. Record owner approval of the public content.
6. Test the agreed common questions, unknown prices, medical-advice boundaries, failures and request workflow using synthetic details.
7. Agree on pilot duration, support hours, usage limits and cancellation terms. Define measurable outcomes such as requests reviewed and response correctness. Do not invent baseline savings.
8. Before receiving real customer details, complete the production security, privacy, retention and contractual work in SECURITY.md with the business owner.
9. Choose a paid operating plan only after estimating AI usage, hosting, support and any messaging costs. Revenue and subscriptions are not guaranteed by the product.

## Subscription billing boundary

There is no automated checkout, recurring billing, self-service signup or customer provisioning in this MVP. A first pilot can be onboarded manually under an agreed scope. Payment automation is a later feature.
